My career in HR began in recruitment and grew through organizational development, compensation and benefits, and people analytics. Each step brought me closer to the connection between employee experiences and organizational decisions. Today, I work as an Employee Experience Specialist at Hamrah Aval (MCI).

What happens after employees give feedback? This question sits at the center of my work. I focus on how employee voice becomes insight, how insight informs a decision, and how that decision leads to action and follow-up.

I approach AI in HR through practical use cases: supporting employee listening, analyzing feedback and improving everyday workflows. I start with a clear problem and a focused pilot, then assess usefulness, conversation quality and the need for human review before considering a wider rollout.

Selected projects.

The context, the work and the value behind three projects.

01Employee Listening & Survey AnalyticsANALYTICS+

Context / ProblemEmployee feedback is only useful when HR teams can quickly see where the experience needs attention and why.

What I builtA reporting and analysis workflow combining quantitative survey results with open-text themes, including onboarding and employee experience feedback.

My role / focusSurvey structure, data preparation, dashboard design, qualitative analysis and management insights.

ValueHelping HR teams move faster from employee feedback to clearer priorities and action discussions.

02HRBP Quarterly Performance & Action TrackingINTERACTIVE PROTOTYPE+

Context / ProblemQuarterly HRBP reviews need more than a final score; managers also need evidence, follow-up actions and visibility into outcomes.

What I builtAn interactive prototype bringing quarterly evaluation, supporting evidence, action tracking and outcome follow-up into one workflow.

My role / focusEvaluation logic, dashboard structure, workflow design, action tracking and UX prototyping.

ValueCreating a clearer distinction between “implementation completed” and “outcome achieved,” while maintaining traceability across review periods.

03AI-Assisted Employee VoiceAI PILOT DESIGN+

Context / ProblemTraditional surveys provide valuable snapshots, but some employee experiences require more continuous and conversational listening.

What I builtA pilot concept and requirements framework for an AI-assisted employee listening channel using conversational voice/text interactions.

My role / focusUse-case definition, conversation design, pilot requirements, privacy considerations and human-review principles.

ValueTesting whether conversational AI can complement existing employee listening methods before larger-scale implementation.
